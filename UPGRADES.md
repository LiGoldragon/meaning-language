# Upgrades

How to deploy each breaking change of meaning-language.

## 0.2.0 → 0.3.0: the 0.33 stack; scalar declarations are newtypes

What breaks:

- Generated by ethos-zero 5a5daca on datom-codec eabead4 and protos 3c7ade3 (the
  0.33 stack). Every scalar declaration that was a type alias is now a one-field
  tuple struct, eighteen in all: `OpaqueMeaning`, `ContentChecksum`, `Locator`, `Dhatu`,
  `Bearer`, `AnnotationTarget`, `DimensionLink`, `KarakaFiller`, `SubpartLink`,
  `RootStatement`, `Karman`, `Samanya`, `Visesa`, `Samavaya`, `Abhava`, `IntentionHole`,
  `RetentionSpecification`, `QualityDomain`. Rust code that built them from a `String`, a
  `ContentLink` or a `Vec` directly now wraps the value, for example `Dhatu("jñā".into())`
  and `Bearer(link)`; reading a field unwraps with `.0`.
- The eleven newtypes over a `String` or another such newtype (`OpaqueMeaning`,
  `ContentChecksum`, `Locator`, `Dhatu`, `Karman`, `Samanya`, `Visesa`, `Samavaya`,
  `Abhava`, `IntentionHole`, `RetentionSpecification`) also derive `PartialOrd` and
  `Ord`. The seven over a compound type (`Bearer`, `AnnotationTarget`, `DimensionLink`,
  `KarakaFiller`, `SubpartLink`, `RootStatement` over `ContentLink`, and `QualityDomain`
  over a `Vec`) do not.
- Compatibility with 0.2.0 is shown by sampled witnesses only, in
  `tests/previous_release.rs`: the datom text of seven values (a link with and without a
  locator, a `Karman`, a `Domain` quality structure, a `Guna` quality, an annotation, an
  `Act`), produced by the 0.2.0 release (provenance in
  `tests/previous_release_provenance.md`) and compared after whitespace normalization,
  written and read back equal; and the archive bytes of six wrappers (`OpaqueMeaning`,
  `Dhatu`, `ContentChecksum`, `Bearer`, `KarakaFiller`, `QualityDomain`) equal to those of
  what they wrap. No claim is made about the other values or types, or that no stored
  format changed.
- `protos::ReaderBudget` has a private field in protos 3c7ade3; build it with
  `ReaderBudget::try_from(nodes)`.
- The flake pins ethos-zero 5a5daca and carries a `flake.lock`.

Known declared consumers: a search of every Cargo.toml under /git/github.com/LiGoldragon
and /home/li/primary for this crate found only meaning-language's own manifest; no other
repository declares it as a dependency, and the only other mentions are coordination notes
under flows/ (527bd4, 90ff48), not code. That covers declared Cargo dependencies, not
unpublished or out-of-tree users.

Checks: `generation-and-datom-roundtrip` (datom feature and glossary coverage; the build
script asserts the checked-in module equals a fresh generation and nothing overwrites it
first), `default-features` (default features only: the datom derives and tests are absent,
the archive test runs) and `generated-equality` (the pinned generator binary on the authored
Ethos, byte for byte against src/generated/meaning.rs).

Deploy:

Repin meaning-language to this release with datom-codec eabead4 and protos 3c7ade3, and
wrap the scalar values as above.

## 0.2.0: generated types archive; datom kinds behind `datom`

What breaks:

- The Rust is now generated by ethos-zero 16.0.0 (c2653dd8), on protos and
  datom-codec 0.32.2. Every generated struct and enum derives rkyv's
  `Archive`, `Serialize` and `Deserialize`; `Datomizable` and `Composing`
  are derived only under the new `datom` feature.
- `protos` is now an optional dependency, enabled by `datom`. datom-codec
  stays unconditional with its `rkyv` feature, because `Quale::Decimal`
  holds its `Decimal`.

To deploy, a consumer that datomizes or reads Datom text into these types
enables `meaning-language/datom`; a consumer that only archives needs
nothing more than rkyv 0.8. The Datom round-trip test runs only under
`datom` (`cargo test --features datom`); the Nix check runs it so.
